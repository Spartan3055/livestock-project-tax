What changed for 2026

Starting with payments made in 2026, the Form 1099-MISC reporting threshold for other income went from $600 to $2,000 (IRS 1099-MISC instructions). Some fairs are now asking families for a W-9 so they can send a 1099-MISC when a youth's auction payments reach $2,000 for the year (Michigan Farm Bureau FAQ). A 1099 does not by itself mean tax is owed, and income below $2,000 is still income. The calculator helps you sort out the numbers.

What the calculator does

Net project income

Auction sale, add-ons, and prizes, minus the animal, feed, vet, supplies, entry fees, and sale commission.

Line 8z statement

A printable statement showing gross income and expenses, the way IRS Publication 225 describes for 4-H and FFA projects.

2026 filing check

Uses the 2026 dependent figures from Rev. Proc. 2025-32: the $1,350 unearned-income line and the earned income plus $450 rule.

“Ask a preparer” flags

Earned vs. unearned income, self-employment tax, the kiddie tax above $2,700, a 1099 that does not match, and a loss.
