How the numbers work

  • Net project income = auction proceeds + add-ons + prizes + other project income, minus project expenses. Pub. 225 says to report a 4-H or FFA project's net income as “Other income” on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8z.
  • Line 8z amount = net project income, or $0 if expenses are larger (and a flag appears).
  • Filing check for a single dependent under 65 and not blind, 2026: a return is required if unearned income is more than $1,350, earned income is more than $16,100, or gross income is more than the larger of $1,350 or earned income (up to $15,650) plus $450. These come from Rev. Proc. 2025-32 and the structure of Pub. 501 Table 2.
  • Kiddie tax flag when unearned income is more than $2,700 (draft 2026 Form 8615 instructions).
  • Self-employment view, only if you tick the trade-or-business box: 92.35% of net income, times 15.3%, when net earnings are $400 or more (Pub. 225, Pub. 501).
