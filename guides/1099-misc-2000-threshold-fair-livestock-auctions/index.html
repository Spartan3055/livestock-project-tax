The New $2,000 1099-MISC Threshold and Youth Livestock Auctions

If your fair asked for a W-9 this year, this is why. The rule that changed is about when the payer must report a payment, not about what is taxable.

What changed

The IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC (Rev. December 2026) say: for tax years beginning after 2025, the minimum threshold for reporting certain payments increased to $2,000, and may be adjusted for inflation beginning in 2027. Box 3 of Form 1099-MISC, “Other income,” is for other income of $2,000 or more that doesn't fit another box, including prizes and awards that are not for services.

Why fairs want a W-9

A W-9 gives the payer the name and taxpayer ID it needs to file a 1099-MISC. The Michigan Farm Bureau's July 2026 FAQ says some fairs are putting new procedures in place, that each fair follows its own process, and that the $2,000 is counted across all qualifying payments in the calendar year, not per auction.

What the 1099 does and doesn't mean

  • A 1099-MISC tells the IRS what was paid. It does not say what is owed. The FAQ puts it plainly: receiving one does not automatically create a tax liability.
  • Expenses still come off. Pub. 225 says to report the net project income on Schedule 1, line 8z, with a statement of gross income and expenses if necessary. That statement is how the 1099 amount and your net figure line up.
  • No 1099 does not mean no income. Payments under $2,000 still count toward the child's income.
  • Check the form. Auction money normally shows in box 3. A 1099-NEC, or a different box, can mean the payer treated it as pay for services. Ask the payer, and ask a preparer.

Timing

If a 1099-MISC is coming for 2026 payments, it should arrive early in 2027. Keep your own record of every check in the meantime so you can match them.

Common questions

What is the 1099-MISC threshold for 2026?

The IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC (Rev. December 2026) say that for tax years beginning after 2025 the minimum reporting threshold increased to $2,000, and it may be adjusted for inflation starting in 2027.

Is the $2,000 per auction or per year?

The Michigan Farm Bureau FAQ says the threshold is based on total qualifying payments received during the calendar year through all participating programs, not per auction.

If my child got less than $2,000, is it tax free?

No. Income can be reportable whether or not a 1099 is issued. Whether a return is needed depends on net project income and the child's other income.
