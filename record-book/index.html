What's inside

  • Project info: animal, tag, purchase date and price, fair dates.
  • Weigh-ins: average daily gain worked out for you.
  • Feed log: pounds and cost, with cost per pound of gain.
  • Expenses: dated entries sorted into animal, feed, vet, supplies, entry fees, commission, and other.
  • Income: auction sale, add-ons, prizes, and other payments, with payer and whether a 1099 is expected.
  • 1099 check: compares each payer's 1099 to what you recorded.
  • Tax summary: net income, the 2026 dependent filing check, and the same “ask a preparer” flags as the calculator.
  • Statement: a print-ready line 8z statement.
  • Short PDF guide: how to fill it in and what to bring to a preparer.

Works in Google Sheets (File > Import, or upload to Drive and open), Excel, and LibreOffice. Sample numbers are labeled as examples; clear them before you start.

What it is not

It is a record book and a worksheet, not tax software, and it does not decide how your child's income should be treated. The earned-versus-unearned question stays a question for a tax preparer.
